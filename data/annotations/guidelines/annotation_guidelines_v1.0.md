# Annotation Guidelines v1.0

## Spam Detection

### spam

Definition:
Unwanted promotional, scam, phishing, or irrelevant email.

Positive Examples:

* "You won a free iPhone"
* "Claim your lottery reward now"

Negative Examples:

* Real customer support emails
* Team communication emails

---

### ham

Definition:
Legitimate normal email communication.

Positive Examples:

* Meeting discussion
* Customer inquiry
* Billing support email

Negative Examples:

* Fake offers
* Scam emails

---

# Priority Labels

## Critical

Definition:
Requires immediate action to avoid serious business impact.

Examples:

* Server outage
* Legal notice
* Security breach

---

## High

Definition:
Important issue needing quick response but not emergency.

Examples:

* Customer complaint
* Delayed payment issue

---

## Medium

Definition:
Normal business communication.

Examples:

* Status update
* General discussion

---

## Low

Definition:
Non-urgent informational message.

Examples:

* Newsletter
* FYI update
